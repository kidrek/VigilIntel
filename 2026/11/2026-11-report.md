# 🇬🇧 Monthly Synthesis — November 2026

## Threat Level

**🔴🔴🔴🔴🔴 5/5 — Severe**

November 2026 was a severe, multi-vector month. Four consecutive weeks combined record vulnerability volume (over 260 CVEs in week 4 alone), confirmed exploitation of edge and virtualization systems (Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466, VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890, Ivanti ICS CVE-2026-111357/CVE-2026-104935, SonicWall CVE-2026-19868, OpenSSH CVE-2026-123158, N-able CVE-2026-90436, Drupal CVE-2026-132165), and industrial-scale data exposure (Expedia 830M claimed, Binance 335M claimed plus $6.3B theft, CSCTelecom 125M, Amplifon 80M, Arara 77M, Maileva 22M, Keio 11M, Tower Records 10.2M, KEPCO 1.52M). Ransomware and RaaS remained the dominant impact model (Qilin, Akira, Play, n0n, Scarlettgroup, The Gentlemen, INC/Lynx, Rhysida), with double extortion, leak-site fragmentation and third-party vendor breaches. State-linked operations intensified: Russia's Turla and COLD RELIC/Star Blizzard, China's Storm-0940, CopperDragon, SilentAir and Webworm, Iran's Volant Manticore, MuddyWater and GreenJuliett/CyberAv3ngers, and North Korea's TraderTraitor, Kimsuky and Sapphire Sleet. The month's strategic inflection point is AI-accelerated offense: agentic frameworks (ARTEX, Cairn, PentAGI) automated reconnaissance, exploit development and credential validation; Google confirmed autonomous zero-day discovery and exploitation compromising 11 organisations in 26 seconds; machine-speed payroll diversion hit Workday in ~33 seconds median. OT sabotage of refrigeration and cold-chain controllers in France and Europe produced physical effects. Identity remained the main bypass surface: AiTM phishing, session-token theft, OAuth/application-token abuse and infostealer-to-cloud chains defeated SMS/TOTP MFA. Regulatory pressure shifted from guidance to enforcement: NYDFS $6.75M settlements, Italian Garante €805M Meta fine, Irish DPC €11M Tinder fine, France's NIS2 resilience bill, CRA guidance, HIPAA changes and India DPDP Rule 7. Priority actions: FIDO2/WebAuthn everywhere, 72-hour patch SLAs for internet-facing edge/virtualization, Tier-0 identity governance including AI agents, OT/ICS segmentation, SBOM/provenance, immutable backups, and machine-speed detection.

## Executive Summary

November 2026 marked a structural escalation: the threat level stayed Severe all month, driven by mass exploitation of internet-facing edge, identity and virtualization systems, industrial-scale data theft, state-linked OT sabotage and AI-accelerated offense. Record vulnerability pressure late in the month (260+ CVEs in five days, 14 CISA KEV additions) saw active exploitation of Ivanti ICS, PHP2Shell, WordPress, OpenSSH SSHole, SonicWall, Cisco Expressway, VMware vCenter, N-able, ScreenConnect, Apache, Drupal, SAP, Fortinet, F5, NGINX, LiteLLM and Danfoss refrigeration controllers. Ransomware/RaaS remained prolific and fragmented—Qilin, Play, Akira, RansomHouse, n0n, INC/Lynx, Chaos, Scarlettgroup, The Gentlemen, Pixelleaks, Dark Project—with double extortion against healthcare, education, manufacturing, retail and public services. Data exposure was industrial: billions of credentials in unprotected databases and claimed breaches of Expedia (830M), Binance (335M), CSCTelecom (125M), Amplifon (80M), Arara (77M), MUJI (27M), Maileva (22M), Eli Lilly (16M), Keio (11M), Tower Records (10M), KEPCO (1.5M) and 1Password (1M), often via third-party processors. Nation-state activity intensified: Russia's Turla, Star Blizzard/COLD RELIC and APT28 ran espionage and sabotage preparation; China's Storm-0940/ARTEX, CopperDragon, SilentAir, Armory Castle and Webworm combined agentic intrusion, SS7 interception, vCenter zero-day exploitation and customised SoftEther backdoors; Iran was publicly attributed the sabotage of ~700 French refrigeration sites and used Volant Manticore's IPFS-based KuboShell; North Korea's TraderTraitor, Kimsuky and Sapphire Sleet pursued crypto theft, fake personas and blockchain dead drops. Identity was the main battleground: AiTM phishing, session-token theft, OAuth/application-identity abuse and MSP delegation bypassed MFA, reinforcing FIDO2 and token binding. AI crossed a threshold as weapon and target: agentic frameworks (ARTEX, Cairn, PentAGI) automated reconnaissance and exploitation; Google confirmed autonomous zero-day discovery and 11 organisations compromised in 26 seconds; LLMjacking, malicious models/npm packages, prompt leakage and machine-speed payroll diversion exposed the AI supply chain. Systemic risks: OT/ICS exposure, software supply-chain compromise and third-party data concentration. Priorities: 72-hour patching of exposed edge systems, phishing-resistant MFA, Tier-0 identity governance, AI-agent/model supply-chain controls, OT segmentation, immutable backups and NIS2/CRA/GDPR breach readiness.

### PART 2 — Geographic Analysis

### Russia

Russia-aligned activity combined espionage, sabotage preparation and ransomware. Turla's Cereal toolset turned government Exchange servers in Kazakhstan and Romania into covert collection hubs. COLD RELIC/Star Blizzard reused prior compromise data to impersonate colleagues and target government, defence and NGO personnel across Europe and North America. APT28/Swallowtail targeted NATO defence industrial and police entities with Hookedge. Akira, Qilin, Play and Everest sustained double extortion. Polish and Estonian authorities reported sabotage preparation and hybrid operations.

### China

China-aligned clusters industrialised espionage and edge exploitation. Storm-0940 used the ARTEX agentic framework and ConnectWise cloud application abuse. CopperDragon's Sparrow framework linked SilentDelivery, OceanGate and DarkWire across telecom, government, defence and research. SilentAir and LIMINAL PANDA intercepted SS7/Diameter signalling to bypass SMS 2FA. Webworm rebuilt SoftEther VPN as NtMonitor for long-dwell government espionage. Armory Castle exploited VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890 as a zero-day.

### Iran

Iran-aligned activity produced physical effects through OT. France attributed October attacks on more than 700 supermarket refrigeration controllers to Shahid Kaveh, an IRGC-CEC unit, after defrost-cycle manipulation caused fires, equipment destruction and food losses. GreenJuliett/CyberAv3ngers scanned FortiGate OT edges and targeted PLCs. Volant Manticore, assessed as MOIS-affiliated and a MuddyWater splinter, used KuboShell over IPFS, Homa post-exploitation and AI-generated fake CV personas against Saudi and Cypriot entities.

### North Korea

North Korean actors continued financial theft and resilient C2. TraderTraitor was attributed the $6.3B Binance theft and targeted crypto exchanges, DevOps engineers and supply chains. Kimsuky used fake Zoom/Webex installers, LNK/ZIP phishing and hidden PowerShell. Sapphire Sleet/BlueNoroff/APT38 used FaceStealer webcam social engineering and IT-worker schemes. UAT-12643 blended blockchain dead drops with targeted infostealing.

### United States

The United States was both a major target and policy driver. FBI NCIC, CIA/ODNI OSINT systems, FinCEN, DHS, USDA, West Virginia Legislature, myColorado and hospitals were reportedly or confirmedly affected. CISA added multiple KEV entries but faced workforce and budget uncertainty. DOJ seizures and indictments targeted North Korean crypto theft, AI voice fraud and university cryptojacking. Policy instability around CMMC and CISA reauthorisation created strategic uncertainty.

### Other

The broader ecosystem remained dominated by ransomware fragmentation and cybercrime. n0n claimed 179 victims and leaked data tied to JPMorgan Chase, Visa and Mastercard through marketing vendors. Scarlettgroup published seven victims in seven days, including CHU de Bordeaux and Sunnybrook Health Sciences. The Gentlemen, Pixelleaks, Rhysida, INC/Lynx, Dark Project, Inception and LeakedData targeted law firms, real estate, education and healthcare. Latin America faced Blind Eagle judicial lures and MedusaLocker against DIAN Colombia.

## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Enforce FIDO2 Phishing-Resistant MFA Everywhere**
   SMS/TOTP MFA was bypassed throughout November by AiTM kits (EvilTokens, Knight Office, Kali365), session-token theft and infostealer-to-cloud chains used by COLD RELIC, Storm-0940 and ShinyHunters. Deploy FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys (YubiKey 5, Google Titan) for all privileged, cloud, VPN, RMM and CI/CD accounts, and revoke all active sessions on rollout. Prioritise Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Workday and ConnectWise access.

2. **Patch Internet-Facing Edge and Virtualization Within 72 Hours**
   Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466, VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890, Ivanti ICS CVE-2026-111357/CVE-2026-104935, SonicWall CVE-2026-19868, OpenSSH CVE-2026-123158, N-able CVE-2026-90436 and Drupal CVE-2026-132165 were actively exploited. Establish a 72-hour SLA for KEV-listed edge, VPN, RMM and virtualization assets, with emergency change approval and virtual patching where immediate upgrade is impossible. Track exposure continuously and report exceptions to the board.

### PARTIE 2 — Analyse Géographique

### Russie

L'activité alignée sur la Russie a combiné espionnage, préparation de sabotage et rançongiciel. Le toolset Cereal de Turla a transformé des serveurs Exchange gouvernementaux au Kazakhstan et en Roumanie en hubs de collecte furtifs. COLD RELIC/Star Blizzard a réutilisé des données de compromissions antérieures pour usurper des collègues et cibler des personnels gouvernementaux, de défense et d'ONG en Europe et en Amérique du Nord. APT28/Swallowtail a ciblé des entités industrielles de défense et de police de l'OTAN avec Hookedge. Akira, Qilin, Play et Everest ont poursuivi la double extorsion. Les autorités polonaises et estoniennes ont signalé préparation de sabotage et opérations hybrides.

### Chine

Les clusters alignés sur la Chine ont industrialisé l'espionnage et l'exploitation de bordure. Storm-0940 a utilisé le framework agentique ARTEX et l'abus d'applications cloud ConnectWise. Le framework Sparrow de CopperDragon reliait SilentDelivery, OceanGate et DarkWire sur les télécoms, le gouvernement, la défense et la recherche. SilentAir et LIMINAL PANDA ont intercepté la signalisation SS7/Diameter pour contourner la 2FA SMS. Webworm a reconstruit SoftEther VPN en NtMonitor pour de l'espionnage gouvernemental de longue durée. Armory Castle a exploité VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890 en zero-day.

### Iran

L'activité alignée sur l'Iran a produit des effets physiques via l'OT. La France a attribué les attaques d'octobre contre plus de 700 contrôleurs de réfrigération de supermarchés à Shahid Kaveh, unité de l'IRGC-CEC, après des manipulations de cycles de dégivrage ayant causé incendies, destructions d'équipements et pertes alimentaires. GreenJuliett/CyberAv3ngers a scanné les passerelles FortiGate OT et ciblé des PLC. Volant Manticore, évalué comme affilié au MOIS et scission de MuddyWater, a utilisé KuboShell via IPFS, le post-exploitation Homa et des personas de faux CV générées par IA contre des entités saoudiennes et chypriotes.

### Corée du Nord

Les acteurs nord-coréens ont poursuivi le vol financier et le C2 résilient. TraderTraitor a été attribué au vol de 6,3 Md$ chez Binance et a ciblé exchanges crypto, ingénieurs DevOps et chaînes d'approvisionnement. Kimsuky a utilisé de faux installateurs Zoom/Webex, du phishing LNK/ZIP et PowerShell caché. Sapphire Sleet/BlueNoroff/APT38 a utilisé l'ingénierie sociale FaceStealer par webcam et des schémas de travailleurs IT. UAT-12643 a mêlé dead drops blockchain et infostealing ciblé.

### États-Unis

Les États-Unis ont été à la fois une cible majeure et un moteur politique. Le NCIC du FBI, les systèmes OSINT de la CIA/ODNI, le FinCEN, le DHS, l'USDA, la législature de Virginie-Occidentale, myColorado et des hôpitaux ont été affectés selon les informations disponibles. La CISA a ajouté plusieurs entrées KEV mais fait face à des incertitudes d'effectifs et de budget. Les saisies et inculpations du DOJ ont visé le vol nord-coréen de crypto, la fraude vocale par IA et le cryptojacking universitaire. L'instabilité autour de CMMC et de la réautorisation de la CISA a créé une incertitude stratégique.

### Autre

L'écosystème élargi est resté dominé par la fragmentation rançongicielle et la cybercriminalité. n0n a revendiqué 179 victimes et divulgué des données liées à JPMorgan Chase, Visa et Mastercard via des prestataires marketing. Scarlettgroup a publié sept victimes en sept jours, dont le CHU de Bordeaux et Sunnybrook Health Sciences. The Gentlemen, Pixelleaks, Rhysida, INC/Lynx, Dark Project, Inception et LeakedData ont ciblé cabinets d'avocats, immobilier, éducation et santé. L'Amérique latine a subi les leurres judiciaires de Blind Eagle et MedusaLocker contre la DIAN en Colombie.

- Confiance dans la seule signature de code pour les installeurs tiers

- Cycles de patch manuels incapables de tenir les échéances KEV
- Réseaux OT/IoT non segmentés avec contrôleurs exposés sur Internet
- NTLM et authentification héritée sans signature ni blocage
